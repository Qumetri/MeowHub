Tracked coins

Removing a coin deletes its price targets and volatility rule too. Its stored candles are left alone, so adding it back restores the chart history.

The Feed column is re-checked against the exchange every hour. A pair can be halted long after you add it — the exchange keeps serving the price it froze at, so a coin marked halted is showing a dead number and cannot alert.

Fluctuation alerts

An alert when price rises or falls by Step within the window — measured from the window's low or high, so a sharp V counts too. While the move keeps going you get another alert at every further step. Once it passes Urgent the alert is sent Pings times, a minute apart, each with the live price. Urgent moves ignore quiet hours.

Telegram bot

  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather → /newbot → copy the token.
  2. Paste it below and press Save.
  3. Send your new bot any message (say hi), then press Detect chat.
  4. Press Send test to confirm, then switch alerts on.

Quiet hours suppress volatility alerts only. A price target you set deliberately always fires.
